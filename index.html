Twebwe

Urujeni Culture Troupe ni itsinda rigamije guteza imbere umuco n'impano z'urubyiruko binyuze mu kubyina, kuririmba no gukorera hamwe.

💃

Kubyina

Guteza imbere impano zo kubyina no kwerekana imbyino z'umuco.

🎤

Kuririmba

🇷🇼

Umuco
